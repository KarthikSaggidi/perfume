## Desktop prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one exceptionally detailed photorealistic luxury perfume website HERO BACKGROUND, landscape 2560x1440 pixels. A dreamlike sunlit ivory marble archway opening onto misty Mediterranean mountains, warm peach morning light and rippling shallow water, pink peony and rose petals floating lightly. Main subject on the RIGHT HALF at 64% width: a large, exquisite rectangular faceted transparent blush-pink perfume bottle tilted subtly clockwise resting on dark stone with pink peonies and roses at its base, cylindrical black finely textured cap with gold rim. On bottle front, elegant restrained gold serif lettering, exact text 'AURÉALIS' and below it 'VELVET BLOOM', tiny 'EAU DE PARFUM'. Keep LEFT 42% airy pale ivory, soft cream haze, quiet and uncluttered with very few objects, suitable for overlaying black web heading. A tall grand marble arch frames bottle on right; realistic beautiful glass refraction, liquid caustics, reflective water, tiny dew drops, delicately suspended petals, extraordinarily premium photographic art direction. Palette warm ivory, champagne gold, peach, blush pink. All lettering confined to bottle label. No slogans, no buttons, no interface, no UI, no collage, no borders, no watermark. Render entire scene edge to edge. Crisp full HD detail, luminous and clean rather than dark.

Prioritize the full bottle visible in LOWER right quarter rather than huge cropped close-up.
